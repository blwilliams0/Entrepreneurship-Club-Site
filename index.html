What this club actually is

Members build businesses, software, creative projects, and work worth putting on a resume. They meet ambitious people and get practical help.

  • People who buildMeet students who are working on real things, and find people to work with.
  • Practical helpGuides, workshops, and feedback aimed at the next step, not at theory.
  • A place to startShow up with an idea, a business you already run, or only curiosity. No business required.
